# EMF 
## A long weekend of geekery in an internet-connected field.

From the [Badgethon](https://bobyn.uk/Badgeathon.html) I won two tickets to attend [EMF](https://www.emfcamp.org/) a biennial festival for all things hacker, maker, artistic, crafty and scientific, a temporary town that posits itself as being for all those with an inquisitive mind. From my experience, I found a place where the culture of creativity and technology goofed off, joked around, let its hair down and flourished. It's important to note that EMF is 100% volunteer ran and self organised, there's no big company looking to make a profit and even orga have to buy their own ticket, and the best way to get a guaranteed ticket is to volunteer, give a talk, run a workshop, or build an installation.

# Build a village

A big part of how EMF self organizes is through villages, a village can be based around themes, shared passions and existing communities, pick a point on the map and declare that you will be pitching your tent there(ish) and anyone interested in your theme will know where to go to meet like minded people. A few of my favourite villages include:
- [Gladstonledburshire on sever](https://www.emfcamp.org/villages/2026/28-glastonledburyshire-on-severn): What do you get when you steal the uk gov branding and start annexing other villages.
- [Scottish consulate](https://www.emfcamp.org/villages/2026/37-scottish-consulate): Scotland's "official" presence.
- [Milliways](https://www.emfcamp.org/villages/2026/35-milliways): Partied the latest and feeding the hungry.

But there are so many more, from biohackers to lockpickers along with just about every hackerspace in the country.

# A festival with proper networking
Any festival you go to these days you can assume the network will be congested beyond use and you won't be able to phone your friends and you'll be perpetually lost and separated, but that just wouldn't stand for this kind of crowd. EMF spins up it's own infrastructure with a comprehensive network for wifi, GSM, LTE and DECT phones and if that wasn't enough they go to the trouble of setting up a plain old telephone service with public phones dotted around the festival that you can even get your own landline phone wired into.

# Talks & Workshops
From my experience at other more technical conferences, talks and workshops can often be overwhelming and EMF is no different, but the real value of getting together like this is the people, so I tend to float around following friends to things of their own interest so I can broaden my horizons and find somthing new while being grateful to the people that do the hard work of recording it all to watch at home, but I do still try to go to a few things that pique my interest in a given day, at EMF I focused on learning new skills like embroidery!
The talks and workshops truly had somthing for everyone, a few personal standouts include:

- Blacksmithing experiances.
- Talk on DnD style alingment chart of paving stones.
- How to brew your own HRT.
- Running a repair cafe.
- Embroidery workshops.
- Why the hacker scene is so queer.
- How to build your own satellite ground station workshop.
- How to add pockets to clothes workshop.
- A fascinating talk about making games for elderly people "It challenged my idea of what games are and can be".

The most popular talk of the whole event was "[breaking into buildings with boobs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rg-rroxA6ho)" a fascinating talk about a career in proffesional security testing that focused on the ways that social engineering and mysogonistic assumptions can easily get you access to otherwise restricted areas and grow your collection of company branded mugs.

## Swap shop
We've all been there, odd bits of wire, old computers, forgotten hardware, whatever it is, there's a home for it as the [swap shop](https://www.emfcamp.org/about/swap-shop), a place where people bring the random junk that’s been sitting in drawers, and go home with some different random junk which just might get turned into something amazing. Of course, what's a pile of electrical odds and ends without EMFs very own [orphan source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n46yBXnWdxQ&vl=hi).

## The Data emporium
The [Data Emporium](https://www.emfcamp.org/installations/2026/561) is where all the data lives, there's data everywhere in our modern world, but somtimes it can get a bit lonely, so the data emporium was a lovely place to collect it all together in one place and to talk about the joy of counting, noting, and just knowing about data.

## Nullsector
What's a festival without a little nightlife? EMF goes all out with a fully themed club which this year took the form of the polybius biotech spaceport set in the year 4242 featuring a number of shipping containers with their own exhibits

### The music

The base was pounding, the robotic cocktail machine was overflowing and the energy every night was electric and the closing night was an unforgettable end that I cannot recount in polite company.

## *The* Badge

The concept of a conference badge has grown from a humble beginning - spawned at DEFCON - to a whole host of crazy devices that only nominally resemble a badge and can often look more like full blown computers in their own rights (could the outpace the apollo computers?) and are generally a wonderful opportunity to create, play around, exchange ideas and showoff. EMF of late has been using the same core for their badges which gives people a lot of lattitude to produce additions for the Tildagon in the form of Hexpansions.

### Hexpansions
Hexpansions can take many forms, they can be inert cosmetics, electrical circuits that interact with the core, or both, if you can dream of it (and it has a 1mm edge to fit into the slot) you can make it into a hexpansion. I saw a [dizzying array of hexpansions](https://tildagon.badge.emfcamp.org/hexpansions/), plastic batwings, lightup cat ears, emergency hotsauce dispeners, GPS modules, SD card slots, hungry hungry hippos games, protoboards and even full on robot conversions with my beloved omniwheels.

# In closing
This has been an attempt to explain EMF and inspire people to go themselves, honestly I barely scratched the surface of what it is and what it's like to *be* there, I could go on and on about the things that didn't make the list but then I'd never finish this blog! Truly it is impossible to image without experiencing and I hope reading about my experiences means I get to meet *you* there next time!.
EMF is an incredible experience, so many passionate inspiring amazing people pushed together into a hot dry field just outside of Ledbury, it's a place to explore new hobbies and new identities, to be welcomed by any and all, to make fleeting acquaintances and lasting connections, from hackerspaces to furries, from robots to blacksmiths, from silly jokes to serious conversations, from new faces to old friends, EMF has it all and if you ever get the opportunity to attend, you would be a fool to turn it down.
